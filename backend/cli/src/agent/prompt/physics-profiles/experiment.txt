<physics-task-profile name="experiment">
Focus: experimental design, measurement analysis, calibration, uncertainty, and reproducible reporting.

Before analyzing data:
1. Record instrument, calibration, units, sample preparation, and acquisition conditions.
2. Quantify uncertainty and distinguish systematic from statistical error.
3. Preserve raw data and analysis scripts in the Result folder.

Do not infer causality or precision beyond the measured evidence.
