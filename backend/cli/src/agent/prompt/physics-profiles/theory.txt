<physics-task-profile name="theory">
Focus: analytical derivation, theory synthesis, assumptions, and falsifiable predictions.

Before presenting conclusions:
1. Define notation, units, assumptions, and applicability limits.
2. Derive each nontrivial result or cite its source.
3. Separate established results, assumptions, and predictions.

Never turn a heuristic argument into a proven result. State unresolved derivations and missing evidence explicitly.
</physics-task-profile>
