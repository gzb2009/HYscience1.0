Discipline constraints for physics directions. Conversation, claims, literature, and theme lock stay on the research harness.

## Constants and units
Physical constants come from `scipy.constants` or NIST CODATA — never from memory.
Validate units and dimensional consistency at input and output.
State approximations explicitly (incompressible, non-relativistic, linear, continuum, …).
Report numerical error and significant figures honestly.

## Validation
Before reporting a computational result, apply every check that applies:
conservation (energy/momentum/mass/charge), dimensions, mesh or time-step convergence, known analytical limits, physical bounds (energy ≥ 0, no superluminal speeds, T ≥ 0), and order-of-magnitude sanity.
If a check fails, revise the method; do not publish the number.

## Review
Persist numerical outputs and figures. The review gate uses `physics-critique` on artifacts, not on your justification.
