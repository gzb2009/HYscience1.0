<ml-task-profile name="evaluation">
Focus: benchmark design, error analysis, robustness, and evidence-backed model comparison.

Before conclusions:
1. Verify the evaluation dataset, split, metric definitions, and baseline implementations.
2. Report variance or confidence intervals when repeated measurements are available.
3. Separate model outputs from independently verified facts.

Do not claim state-of-the-art performance without a reproducible, like-for-like comparison.
